About FitPulse

Fitness is easier when you can see your effort.

Why we built it

Most people don't quit because workouts are hard. They quit because they can't see their progress. FitPulse gives you one place to log sessions, note how they felt, track your weight and hear back from a coach.

How it works

Member logs a workout → Admin assigns a coach → Coach reviews it and leaves remarks → Member checks the feedback → Progress keeps building.

Who uses it

🙋

Member

Logs workouts, tracks weight and reads coach remarks.

🧑‍🏫

Coach

Reviews assigned workouts, updates status and gives tips.

🛠️

Admin

Manages members, coaches, assignments and reports.
